## Table 3. Summary of Moderator Evidence

> **Notes:**
> - Studies were grouped by baseline WM level (Low vs. High/NR) and outcome domain.
> - Evidence strength: Consistent = >75% of studies in agreement; Moderate = 50–75%; Mixed = <50%; Insufficient = <3 studies.
> - Cognitive process overlap was operationalized per Barnett & Ceci (2002): High = same WM storage/updating mechanisms; Medium = partially overlapping executive functions (e.g., inhibition, shifting); Low = distinct processes (episodic memory, processing speed, everyday functioning).
